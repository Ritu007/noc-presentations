Updated CHI Router Architecture

  • The VA stage is replaced by a Resource Allocation (RA) stage, which checks the downstream credit availability.
  • If credits are available, the resource is allocated to one of the available flits in the buffers (FIFOs), based on QoS and age.
  • QoS, the CHI handshake and the CHI-based credit mechanism are integrated.
  • A proposed age-based priority algorithm avoids flit starvation.

Router Pipeline

The pipeline and flow-control unit for a single CHI channel. The other channels follow the same pipeline.
